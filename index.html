The constraint

The AI build-out is short of one thing. We supply it.

PacinfraX develops AI data centers mainly in the region of low cost energy source and builds the power generation that energizes them. Power is the constraint on artificial intelligence, and we solve it on the site itself — gas and storage delivering compute capacity that does not wait in an interconnection queue.

The Platform

One integrated system, not nine procurements.

Compute, generation, storage and recovery are selected together and engineered to operate as a single system. Each element earns its place by what it contributes to delivered cost per megawatt-hour and to the schedule on which capacity comes online.

That integration is the product. Buying the same nine technologies separately gives you nine vendors, nine warranties and nine schedules — and no guarantee they work as one.

Services

From design through delivered product

Three capabilities that work as one line: engineering that defines the part, manufacturing that builds it, and the supply chain services that move it from our floor to your site.

01

Engineering

We take a specification and make it manufacturable — or take a concept and make it a specification.

  • Design and development — mechanical and electrical design for energy hardware and enclosures
  • Design for manufacture — value engineering to reduce part count, cycle time and unit cost
  • Prototyping — short-run builds for fit, function and field trial
  • Qualification and testing — verification against the standards your insurer and lender will ask about
02

Manufacturing

Production operated to client specification, with the quality systems and reporting industrial buyers require.

  • Contract manufacturing — dedicated or shared capacity against your volume profile
  • Precision fabrication — sheet metal, machining and welded assemblies for energy equipment
  • Assembly and integration — mechanical, electrical and controls integration into finished units
  • Quality systems — documented inspection, traceability and first-article approval
03

Supply chain

The services that connect engineering to delivery — and keep a project schedule from becoming a procurement problem.

  • Strategic sourcing — qualified vendor identification across Asia and North America
  • Vendor management — audits, corrective action and performance reporting
  • Logistics and freight — containerised shipment, customs and inland transport to site
  • Inventory and fulfilment — staging, kitting and release against a construction schedule

Production Base

Vietnam: from assembly hub to industrial platform

Vietnam has become one of the most strategically positioned mid-scale industrial platforms in the world — and it is where our production base sits.

Scale

A core node in global supply chains

Electronics contributes over 35% of total exports, with computers, electronics and components surpassing $100 billion for the first time. Vietnam now supplies roughly 30% of US smartphone imports — second only to China.

Momentum

The China+1 destination of choice

Manufacturing and processing captured $8.06 billion of registered FDI in the first five months of 2026, with registered capital up 34.9% year on year as multinationals diversify production bases away from single-country concentration.

Depth

Moving up the value chain

Samsung, LG, Foxconn, Pegatron, BOE and Intel have built extensive production bases in Bac Ninh, Hai Phong and Thai Nguyen. The shift is under way from final assembly toward printed circuit boards, sensors and optical components.

Policy

Structural incentives

Corporate income tax holidays, import-duty exemptions and VAT refunds, land-lease incentives with partial co-financing of industrial-park infrastructure, and fast-track procedures for high-tech and green projects.

Talent

An engineering pipeline

Government targets 50,000 trained semiconductor engineers by 2030, with universities launching chip-design programs to attract FDI from Japan, South Korea and the US.

Advantage

What this gives our projects

A qualified supplier base for mechanical and cooling equipment, containerized shipment to Gulf Coast ports, and a second sourcing route when Western equipment queues run to 52–80 weeks.
